Lesson Reflection

1. What have I learned in this lesson?

Use this box to record what the student feels they learned.

2. What did I find easy?

Capture what felt manageable, successful or straightforward.

3. What did I find hard?

Record anything the student found tricky, frustrating or confusing.

4. What help do I need to do it better?

Use this to note support, resources or teaching changes the student needs.

Each reflection question can be answered separately by typing or using the record button. Speech-to-text works best in Chrome or Edge, but may work in some Safari setups too.
